Fixed-path PERT and Monte Carlo

The approximation assumes independent activity durations and one fixed critical path. Simulation allows the controlling path to change.

Simulation draws independent Normal durations with the entered means and variances, then floors each draw at 0.1 day. This is a teaching assumption; flooring changes the distribution's moments.

A 60-minute classroom sequence

  1. Predict · 0–10 min. Turn off Reveal solutions. Draw the network and predict its critical path.
  2. Build the logic · 10–20 min. Discuss parallel activities and why merge points wait for every predecessor.
  3. Reveal · 20–35 min. Calculate early and late dates, then interpret slack.
  4. Challenge · 35–50 min. Try E = 7, H = 10, A = 18, and E = 3. Predict each result first.
  5. Explore risk · 50–60 min. Compare fixed-path PERT with simulated completion and criticality frequencies.

Discussion questions

Why is A not critical when F requires it? How much delay can H absorb? When does shortening one activity stop helping? Why might the mean simulated completion exceed the fixed-path mean?
